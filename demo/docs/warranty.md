# Warranty

## Tents and shelters

Tents carry a 5-year warranty against seam failure, broken poles and zipper defects. The warranty does not cover UV damage from long-term pitching, damage from wind when guy lines were not used, or mildew from storing the tent wet.

## Backpacks

Backpacks carry a lifetime warranty on stitching and buckles. Fabric wear on the base panel from normal use is not a defect.

## Making a claim

Send three photos of the problem and your order number to warranty@fernwood.example. Most claims are decided within 3 business days. We repair first, replace if repair is not possible, and refund only if neither is.
