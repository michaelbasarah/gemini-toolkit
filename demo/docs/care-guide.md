# Caring for your gear

## Washing a sleeping bag

Wash down sleeping bags in a front-loading machine on a gentle cold cycle with a down-specific soap. Never use fabric softener. Tumble dry on low with two clean tennis balls to break up clumps; this can take three hours or more. Make sure the bag is completely dry before storing it.

## Waterproofing a tent

Re-apply a spray-on waterproofing treatment once a year, or whenever water stops beading on the fly. Do this outdoors on a dry day with the tent pitched. Seam-seal the floor seams at the same time.

## Storing gear

Store tents and sleeping bags loose in a cotton sack, not compressed in their stuff sacks. Keep them somewhere dry and out of direct sunlight.
