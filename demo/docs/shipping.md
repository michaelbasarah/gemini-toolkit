# Shipping

## Domestic delivery

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and costs 6.00. Orders over 75.00 ship free. Express delivery (1 to 2 business days) costs 14.00 and is not available to P.O. boxes.

Orders placed before 14:00 local time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.

## International delivery

We ship to 28 countries. International orders take 7 to 14 business days. Duties and taxes are collected by the carrier on delivery and are not included in our prices.

## Tracking

Every parcel gets a tracking link by email when it leaves the warehouse. If a tracked parcel shows no movement for 5 business days, write to support and we will open a carrier investigation.
