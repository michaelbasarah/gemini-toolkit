# Returns and refunds

Fernwood Outdoor is a made-up shop used for this demo. Its policies exist only so there is something to ask about.

## Refund window

You can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the parcel arriving back at our warehouse.

Items that were worn, washed or used outdoors count as used. We can offer store credit for those at our discretion, but not a cash refund.

## Final-sale items

Anything marked "final sale" at checkout, plus gift cards and custom-engraved bottles, cannot be returned.

## How to start a return

Email returns@fernwood.example with your order number. We reply with a prepaid label within one business day. Return shipping is free inside the country; international returns are at the customer's cost.
